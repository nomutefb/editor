① 작품(주인공) 정체성 락:
A single matte pure-white biomorphic sculpture, about 60 cm tall, standing alone on the floor: a plump egg-like blob whose upper mass curls over and down to its left like a thick soft wave hugging its own body, forming one deep rounded crease that spirals around the form — the silhouette of a chubby comma, or a small creature curled asleep. Seamless smooth plaster-matte surface, soft white with gentle gray shading in its hollows; no face, no eyes, no mouth, no legs. A few tiny dark twig-like stick fragments are embedded in it: one short twig protruding from its lower left flank, and a tiny pair near the center-right of the folded front, like miniature dry-branch hands. It sits on a thin round charcoal-black fabric mat, about 60 cm across, lying flat on the polished floor.

A quiet contemporary art gallery: smooth blue-gray walls washed by big soft oval pools of spotlight, dark gray polished concrete floor with a faint mirror reflection of everything above it. On the wall to the right hangs a separate artwork — one thin black rope running vertically from near the ceiling down to the floor, its lower half strung with many small round beads in white, gray, black, and scattered candy colors (yellow, green, blue, pink, orange), the rope's tail curving slightly where it meets the floor with one stray bead beside it. High on that wall, a small dark artist-name plaque and a few tiny lines of text are visible only far away and softly out of focus. The same wall plane and floor continue seamlessly beyond the frame — an empty, calm, dim exhibition room with no people and no windows.
