## 1. 발사 프롬프트 (Gemini / Nano Banana · 그대로 복사)

```text
cinematic, shot on 35mm film, film grain, filmic color grade

Scene: Emotional focal point: his genuine Duchenne smile — cheeks raised, crow's-feet wrinkles around the eyes — as he holds a thick bundle of Korean 50,000-won banknotes up at chest height with both hands and looks directly into the camera with steady eye contact. A Korean man in his mid-20s, short black hair, wearing a plain short-sleeve summer T-shirt and light cotton trousers, stands on a sunlit city sidewalk in Korea. Directly behind him, parked at the curb, sits a retired Korean city bus converted into a dwelling: curtains and a small air-conditioner unit fitted into the passenger windows, laundry hung on a line inside, a potted plant on the dashboard, a folding step set down at the open door. The long side of the bus carries one large clear sign reading 행복주택, and a second smaller board bolted beside the same door reads 청년취업센터 — the housing and the job centre are the same single vehicle. Ordinary pedestrians walk past in the background without looking.

Camera: medium shot from eye-level, shot on a 35mm classic wide lens, natural documentary perspective, balanced subject and background, minimal distortion; vintage uncoated lens, low contrast, blooming flare, hazy glow, soft veiling; diffusion filter, Black Pro-Mist, bloomed highlights, soft halation

Lighting/mood: harsh midday summer sun, hard shadows, glare, heat shimmer rising off the asphalt

Accent: the yellow of the 50,000-won notes and the city-bus blue of the converted bus are the only saturated colours; everything else stays muted and film-neutral

Composition: foreground / midground / background separation with atmospheric depth — the young man in the foreground, the converted bus immediately behind him filling the midground, the street receding behind that

Text handling: render only the two short Korean signs 행복주택 and 청년취업센터 clearly and legibly; no other readable lettering anywhere in the frame

Aspect ratio: 4:5 vertical portrait framing, full bleed single image filling the entire frame edge to edge, no inner border, no outer frame, no rectangular outline, no white margin
MANDATORY: ONE single seamless photograph of ONE continuous scene — no panel division, no divider line, no frame inside the frame

NEGATIVE — strictly avoid:
- deformed hands, extra fingers, distortion, warped face, watermark, morphing, identity drift
- plastic skin, over-smoothing, waxy face — keep natural skin texture with visible pores
- plastic sheen, over-smoothed surfaces, airbrushed look, fake-glossy render — keep realistic surface texture and natural micro-detail
- over-saturated, HDR look, garish colours, crushed contrast
- oversharpen halos, edge halos, crunchy outlines, ringing artifacts
- duplicated subject, cloned figures, repeated faces, tiled background, copy-paste artifacts
- floating objects, ungrounded subject, no contact shadow, hovering feet
- distorted perspective, warped lines, bent architecture, tilted horizon
- no English text, no garbled letters, no fake script, no dense text, no long signage text
```

### 네거티브 선택의 근거
- **NEG16**(AI 플라스틱 광택)·**NEG11**(피부 플라스틱화)·**NEG27**(오버샤픈 헤일로)·**NEG15**(과채도 HDR) = 「AI 느낌」의 실체 4종. 라이브러리 메모대로 NEG11은 부정문보다 **긍정문**(`keep natural skin texture`)이 효과가 크다 하여 양쪽 다 실었다.
- **NEG05(화질 저하)는 일부러 뺐다** — 그 항목 메모가 "레트로 룩과 충돌 · STYLE05·V06 쓸 땐 빼기(모순 금지)". 필름 그레인을 주문하면서 노이즈를 금지하면 프롬프트가 스스로를 무효화한다. NEG27과 NEG05를 같이 넣지 말라는 주석(PE-06)도 같은 축.
